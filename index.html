IdlePages Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · App package: com.kjbj.beebook

IdlePages (“the app”) turns e-books into page images you can read on your lock screen and home screen. This policy explains what data the app uses and what it does not collect.

1. Summary

IdlePages is designed to keep your books and reading state on your device. We do not require an account, do not run ads in the core experience, and do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Books and page images. Files you import (for example EPUB or PDF) and generated page images are stored in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Preferences. Settings such as font, theme, lock-screen layout margins, reading progress, streak counters, trial or purchase status flags, and feature toggles are saved locally with SharedPreferences (and related local storage).
  • Wallpaper state. If you enable lock-screen wallpaper updates, the app may temporarily keep a backup of your previous lock wallpaper so it can restore it when you turn the feature off.

3. Permissions and device features

  • Notifications. Used for optional page controls (previous / next), the double-flip listening indicator, and an optional daily reading streak reminder. You can deny notification permission; some controls will be unavailable.
  • Accelerometer / foreground service. If you enable “double-flip to change wallpaper,” a foreground service listens for a double face-down / face-up gesture and advances the lock-screen page. Sensor data is processed on-device in real time and is not uploaded.
  • Wallpaper. With your consent via the in-app switch, IdlePages can set lock-screen wallpaper images generated from your books.
  • Home screen widget / Quick Settings tile. Optional shortcuts that change the current page locally; they do not send book content off the device.

4. Network use

Core reading and wallpaper features work offline with files already on your device. Display fonts used by IdlePages are bundled in the app and are not downloaded at runtime. The app may use the network only for:

  • Processing purchases or restoring entitlements through Google Play Billing.
  • Opening links you tap (for example this privacy policy) in your browser.

Book contents, page images, and reading progress are not uploaded to IdlePages servers. IdlePages does not operate its own user-account backend for this app.

5. Purchases

Optional subscriptions or one-time unlocks are handled by Google Play. Google’s privacy policy applies to payment processing. IdlePages stores local entitlement flags so premium features can work on your device.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your books or reading history with advertisers. Data stays on your device except for Play Billing / browser links you open, and any sharing you initiate yourself (for example exporting a file with another app).

7. Children

IdlePages is a general-audience productivity / reading utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete books in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall IdlePages. Uninstalling removes the app’s private files and preferences from the device.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the effective date on this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy for IdlePages (com.kjbj.beebook) can be sent to the developer contact listed on the Google Play store listing for IdlePages.
